# WP13 — Transcript exports and cleanup

## Differences from Android

- **Same writers, same bytes.** `TextExport`, `SubtitleCues`, `SrtWriter`, `VttWriter`, `ExportNames` and
  `ExportClock` (CassiniNotebook `Export/`) are ports of Android's `:core` export package. Android's goldens
  (`meeting.txt`, `meeting.srt`, `meeting.vtt` and the word list `meeting.words.tsv`) are copied unchanged into
  `Packages/CassiniNotebook/Tests/CassiniNotebookTests/Resources/export/`, and `ExportGoldenTests` checks the output
  byte for byte. Lengths are counted in UTF-16 units, as on Android.
- **Where the words come from.** Android exports the words of the sealed document. iOS exports the note's
  `note.json` transcript (`ExportTranscript(note:version:)`): each timed word keeps its range, and a word whose text
  holds several words gives one word per part with the same range, as the sealed document does. Labels are the
  speakers' names as the note shows them; iOS stores names, so there is no placeholder numbering at export time.
- **Edited passages.** A passage edited by hand has no word timing on iOS (Android has no such passage). Its words
  share the passage's span in proportion to their UTF-16 length, one after the other without overlapping
  (`ExportTranscript.untimedWords`), so the usual pause, span, length and sentence rules cut it into cues and lines.
- **Passages without a speaker.** iOS can leave a passage unattributed when no speaker turn covers it. Such words get
  the speaker id `unassigned` (the id the sealed document uses) and the label "Unknown speaker" ("Voce sconosciuta").
  The TXT subtitle line counts the exported transcript's speakers (the note's, or the version's), as Android counts
  the selected variant's, so `unassigned` is never counted as a speaker.
- **Plain notes.** Android's "plain note" (an audio file that is not a document) maps to a note without a transcript:
  the transcript exports need a finished transcription (`status == .ready`, even without words, which writes "No words
  were found in this recording.") or a transcript kept from before. A live capture never exports. As on Android
  (`NoteExports.NOT_READABLE`), an unreadable, invalid-metadata or unknown-format document exports no transcript
  (`TranscriptExports.availability`). While the exports are unavailable the "Export transcript" menu is disabled and
  shows Android's reason under its title ("Available after transcription", "Not available for this transcript"); a
  failed write says "Couldn’t save the file".
- **TXT subtitle line.** Android writes "8 Oct 2026, 15:10 · 23:50 · 3 speakers" with its own date pattern. iOS
  joins the locale's abbreviated date and short time with Android's "%@, %@" pattern, so en_US reads "Oct 8, 2026,
  3:10 PM · 23:50 · 3 speakers". The duration and speaker count parts are Android's.
- **Share sheet instead of a picker.** Android writes into a `content://` destination chosen in the system picker and
  stages the text in `cacheDir/exports/` only during the write. iOS hands a file to the share sheet, so each export is
  written to `tmp/Exports/<uuid>/<name>` and removed once the share sheet is dismissed, shared or cancelled
  (`View.shareSheet(item:)`, `ExportService.discard`, also for the Cassini document export). Backing out of one
  activity (Mail, Messages, an extension) returns to the sheet and keeps the file. The exports folder itself is
  removed with `rmdir`, so only when no other export is in it. A note's own audio ("Share original audio") is shared in
  place and never removed. Exports wait for the launch work to finish, so the launch sweep never removes one that is
  on screen.
- **Launch sweep.** As Android's `DocumentRepository.sweep`, the `sweep` startup step deletes `tmp/Exports`, `*.tmp`
  files older than an hour directly in a note directory, and a note directory without a note that holds nothing but
  such files and is older than an hour. It never deletes audio and never looks into `work/`. The known notes are the ones that loaded, as
  Android's rows; a directory whose `note.json` could not be read is kept because it holds that file, while one left
  with nothing but stale `*.tmp` files (an import that crashed) is removed.
- **JSON export** (iOS extra) is unchanged in content: the note with its speakers' voice links and exclusions
  removed. It now lives in `JSONExport` and exports the chosen version when one is given.
- **Export names.** Saved files use `ExportNames` (reserved characters become `_`, at most 100 UTF-16 units,
  fallback "Cassini note") for transcripts and Cassini documents alike; the old names replaced every symbol with `-`
  and cut at 80 characters.
- **Turns.** `TextExport` groups words into turns with a small internal `TextExport.Turn`, because
  `TranscriptTurns` (and `TranscriptTurnsTest`) belong to WP6 in this plan. It can switch to WP6's type once both land.

## Interface changes

- `TranscriptFormat` no longer has `title` or `data(for:)`. Rendering is `TranscriptExports.data(_:format:version:texts:)`;
  the menu titles are in the app (`ExportCopy.title`, Export table).
- `ExportStore.exportTranscript(_:format:)` takes a note id, is `async` and throws `ExportError`.
- `ShareSheet` takes a `ShareItem`, whose optional `onComplete` runs once the sheet is dismissed; views present it
  with `.shareSheet(item:)`. `ExportStore.shareItem(_:)` makes an export's item.
- `ExportStore.init` takes a `ready` closure (as `ImportStore`); `ExportService.discardFolder(_:)` removes one export
  folder from `newFolder()`.
- `AppEnvironment.exports` (default `tmp/Exports`) names the exports folder; `TestGraph` puts it under its scratch root.
- `Package.swift`: the CassiniNotebookTests target declares its `Resources/export` folder (the goldens).
