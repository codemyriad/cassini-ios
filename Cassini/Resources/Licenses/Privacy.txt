# Privacy

Cassini for iOS keeps your recordings, transcripts and voiceprints on your device. Cassini does not upload them. Cassini has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reports. The app connects to the internet only to download its models, and only after you agree.

## The data that Cassini keeps

Cassini keeps your data in its private app storage (Application Support). Other apps cannot read this storage. Each note has its own folder, `Cassini/<noteId>/`. The `<noteId>` is a random identifier.

- Audio: your recordings and the audio files that you import or open with Cassini. A recording is `capture.caf` while it records, then `audio.m4a`. Imported audio keeps its own file type.
- Transcripts: the words and their times, the speaker labels, the speaker names that you give, the bookmarks and the title, in `note.json`. `provenance.json` records which models made the transcript.
- Unfinished transcription: the words found so far and how far the transcription got, in `work/`. Cassini deletes this folder when the transcription ends, when you start again, or when it deletes the note.
- Voiceprints: a voiceprint is a list of numbers that Cassini calculates from the voice of one speaker. Cassini uses voiceprints to recognise the people that you name. They are kept in `PrivateVoices/` only, outside the note folders.
- Settings and state: your model download decisions and their time, the queue of transcriptions (`jobs.json`), and some one-time notices, in the app's preferences.
- Models: the downloaded models, in `CassiniModels/models/<model>/<revision>/`, each with the model host's notices (`NOTICE.txt`). They contain no personal data. iOS keeps Apple's speech models itself.

When you import a file, or open a file with Cassini from a different app, Cassini copies the file into its storage. The original file stays where it is.

## Network

Cassini connects to the internet only to download models. It sends no audio, transcripts, voiceprints, names or settings.

Cassini downloads no model before you agree. The "Transcribe on this device" screen lists the models it needs and the size of each download. The first download starts only when you tap "Download and transcribe" or "Download". If you tap "Not now — keep the audio", Cassini downloads nothing.

- Speech recognition (Parakeet Ultra and its speech detector, Silero VAD): `dist.gocassini.com`, Cassini's model host. On devices without the memory for Parakeet Ultra, Cassini uses Apple's on-device speech models instead: iOS downloads them from Apple and keeps them, and Cassini asks iOS to install the model for the speech language you choose.
- Telling speakers apart (NVIDIA Nemotron-3-Diarization): `dist.gocassini.com`
- Recognising saved voices (3D-Speaker CAM++): `dist.gocassini.com`

Before a download, Cassini reads the list of models (`manifest.json`) and the model host's notices (`NOTICE.txt`) from the same host. Cassini downloads only the model revisions that this version of Cassini names; it never follows a newer entry of the list.

Cassini downloads models only from this host, only over HTTPS, and follows a redirect only to it, at most five times:

- `dist.gocassini.com`

The models are also checked: each downloaded file and the file that Cassini unpacks from it must match their pinned sizes and SHA-256 hashes before Cassini installs them.

A host receives the IP address of your device and the name of the file. The privacy policy of each host applies to these data. Cassini sends no cookies and no account data.

Before Cassini uses a downloaded model for the first time after it starts, it checks the SHA-256 hash of every file again. A model that fails this check is deleted and has to be downloaded again.

## Permissions

- Microphone: Cassini uses the microphone only while you record. A Live Activity shows the recording on the Lock Screen.
- Notifications: Cassini tells you when a transcription ends or needs you.

## Backup

Your notes are kept in protected app storage and can be included in your iCloud or computer backups. Voiceprints and the downloaded models are excluded from backups.

## Copies that you save

Cassini writes a file outside its storage only when you tell it to, through the share sheet. A Cassini document or an audio file has the audio, the transcripts, the speaker names and the title; the transcript as text, subtitles or JSON has the words, their times and the speaker names. These files contain no voiceprints. Cassini cannot delete a copy that you saved or sent to somebody.

## Deletion

When you move a note to Recently Deleted, Cassini keeps it for 30 days. You can restore it during this time. Cassini deletes it when it opens on or after the 30th day. "Delete now" deletes one note immediately, and "Empty" deletes them all.

When Cassini deletes a note, it deletes its audio, its transcripts, its unfinished transcription and its voiceprints. A saved voice can come from a note that you delete; to remove a saved voice, use "Forget" in Saved voices. "Forget everyone" deletes all voiceprints. After you forget a voice, the speaker names in your notes stay.

If you delete Cassini, iOS deletes all Cassini data, including the downloaded models.
